Selected work
CTO and co-founder, Lunu (2019–2025)
Berlin

Co-founded Lunu and led the technical organisation from concept to a production payments infrastructure bridging crypto and retail. Designed the architecture across the full stack: POS terminals and mPOS software, checkout widget for online merchants, iOS and Android crypto wallets, merchant backend, exchange integrations and pricing oracles. Built and led a 13-person engineering team. At peak: ~400 merchants, over €50M annual processing volume. Two patents, filed and granted, on the crypto-payment processing method.

Paris

Designed and prototyped a blockchain protocol for privacy-preserving digital advertising — anonymized ad targeting with on-device data sovereignty and direct customer-to-advertiser value transfer. Took the concept through architecture, patent filing, team build, and working prototype. Project paused at the seed-funding stage.
